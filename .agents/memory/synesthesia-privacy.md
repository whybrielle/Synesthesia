---
name: Synesthesia privacy boundary
description: The product requirement that photo-derived creations remain local to the user's device.
---

Synesthesia is a private, browser-based tool: photo analysis, melody synthesis, and the user's saved photo-melody library must remain on the device. It should not require accounts or upload photos or creations to a server.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires local-only processing and storage for personal photos.

**How to apply:** Keep future Synesthesia features client-side when they handle photo content or saved creations; do not introduce remote uploads or account requirements.
